AUDIO FILES — Valko Paw Wall
==============================

Place your audio files here. Two tracks are expected:

  hero-ambient.mp3   (+ hero-ambient.ogg as fallback)
  → Plays on the hero section. Full volume at the top of the page,
    fades out smoothly as you scroll down toward the paw wall.

  wall-theme.mp3     (+ wall-theme.ogg as fallback)
  → Plays when approaching the paw wall section. Starts silent at
    the hero, fades in as the paw wall comes into view.

FORMAT TIPS
-----------
- MP3 is fine for most browsers. OGG is an optional fallback.
- Recommended: loop-friendly files (no hard intro/outro, clean loop point)
- Keep file sizes reasonable: ~2–5 MB is good for ambient tracks

If you only have MP3s, the OGG lines in index.html can be ignored —
the browser will just use the MP3.
